## 2. Integrated Workflows Implemented & Verified

### Workflow 1: Customer Self-Registration, GPS Discovery & Dairy Approval
- Mobile farmer registers via Customer App, discovers nearby dairies within GPS radius.
- Registration creates unapproved record (`PENDING_APPROVAL`, status `INACTIVE`).
- E.164 normalization prevents duplicate phone registrations (409 Conflict).
- Dairy Admin reviews registration in `/customers/pending`, assigns canonical `farmer_code = 'C-101'`, and activates account.

### Workflow 2: Face Biometric Enrollment & Kiosk Vector Synchronization
- Enrolls 128-dimensional float embedding generated by SFace/YuNet; validates L2 unit norm ($\approx 1.0$).
- Incremental downstream sync pushes vector to authorized center kiosks without raw imagery.
- Cosine similarity matching ($\ge 0.65$) enables counter identification with $< 15$ ms lookup.
- Fallback hierarchy established: Face Biometric $\to$ RFID Card scan $\to$ Manual Farmer Code entry.

### Workflow 3: Milk Collection Session & Real Hardware Intake
- Operator opens morning/evening shift session; prevents duplicate open shifts (409 Conflict).
- Weighing scale RS-232 parser extracts stable net weight (`12.34 kg`).
- Ultrasonic milk analyzer RS-232 parser extracts FAT, SNF, and calculated Density.
- Deterministic rate calculation computes milk amount with exact commercial rounding.
- Transaction saved with immutable rate snapshot; ESC/POS 40-column multilingual thermal slip generated.
- Shift closed with volume and quality aggregates.

### Workflow 4: Offline Kiosk Storage, Push/Pull Batch Sync & Closed-Session Policy
- Kiosk transactions committed locally to IndexedDB/SQLite with client intake UUID and idempotency key.
- Batch push (`POST /api/v1/sync/push`) commits offline events atomically using PostgreSQL savepoints.
- Downstream pull (`GET /api/v1/sync/pull`) fetches newly approved farmers and RFID cards by incremental cursor.
- **Closed-Session Decision Gate:** Late offline milk intakes arriving after a shift session has closed on the server are preserved, flagged `transaction_status = 'RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED'`, recorded in `sync_conflict_records`, and credited to the farmer ledger with an audit trail.

### Workflow 5: Double-Entry Subledger, Billing Cycles & Maker-Checker Bulk Payout
- Completed milk collection credits `CUSTOMER_PAYABLE` and debits `MILK_PURCHASE_EXPENSE`.
- Feed sales debit `CUSTOMER_PAYABLE` and credit `FEED_INVENTORY`.
- 10-day periodic billing cycle computes net payable: `Gross Milk Earnings - Feed Deductions`.
- Billing cycle finalization transitions bills to `FINALIZED` and locks all period intakes.
- Segregation of Duties: Accountant (Maker) prepares bulk payout batch; Maker cannot approve own batch (403 Forbidden); Dairy Owner (Checker) approves batch.

### Workflow 6: Bank NEFT File Export, Reconciliation & Farmer Passbook Reflection
- Generates 11-column RBI standard NEFT CSV with SHA-256 header and `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- Bank return CSV reconciliation updates successful rows to `SETTLED`, records Bank UTR, and debits `CUSTOMER_PAYABLE`.
- Failed return rows marked `FAILED` without debiting payable balance.
- Customer App passbook streams real-time transaction history with Bank UTR reference.

### Workflow 7: Super Admin Platform Oversight, AMC & Multi-Tenant Security
- Super Admin provisions new Dairy tenant databases and configures database connection pools.
- Global Dairy / Federation Procurement Price Floor policies enforced deterministically.
- Hardware inventory tracking for kiosks, scales, and analyzers with AMC expiry alerts.
- Cross-tenant requests strictly rejected with 403 Forbidden.

### Workflow 8: Event-Driven Notification Delivery
- Dispatches transactional SMS/WhatsApp notifications on key lifecycle events:
  - Registration approval with assigned farmer code.
  - Milk collection intake confirmation with weight, FAT, SNF, and amount.
  - Billing cycle bill generation.
  - Payout disbursement with Bank UTR reference.
- Enforces DLT template registration compliance and quiet-hours window suppression.
